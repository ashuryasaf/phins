01 · The opportunity

A trillion-dollar protection gap — served by software built before the smartphone.

The world is aging and living longer, but the systems that protect people against severe disability and the cost of long-term care are analog, adversarial and underfunded. That mismatch is not a demand problem — it is a software, pricing and trust problem, exactly what an AI-native platform with flawless data integrity is built to solve.

Why people are unprotected

  • Aging + longevity shock widen the private protection gap every year.
  • Severe disability is mispriced or excluded — carriers retreat or overcharge.
  • Claims are adversarial: the moment of need is the moment of friction.
  • Agents are stuck in software from the 1990s, fragmenting the relationship.

Why incumbents can’t fix it

  • Legacy cores bolt AI onto decades-old, unauditable infrastructure.
  • Channel conflict: carriers under-invest in agent software.
  • Balance-sheet capital intensity can’t move at software speed or margin.
  • No data flywheel — underwriting, claims and care data never compound.

04 · Business model

Three compounding, capital-light revenue engines — plus a flywheel that links them.

Engine 1 rate-based
MGA insurance take
A take rate on gross written premium. Risk sits with carriers/reinsurers — capital-light.
Engine 2 recurring
AgentOS SaaS
Per-seat recurring software with high gross margin and a land-and-expand motion.
Engine 3 network
Marketplace take
A take rate on marketplace GMV (care, services, embedded products) with no underwriting risk.

The growth flywheel

  • AgentOS adds agents → they bring their book and clients onto the platform.
  • Clients generate data → each policy and claim enriches proprietary underwriting and claims datasets.
  • AI prices sharper → better loss ratios and treaty terms.
  • Marketplace deepens → care and provider supply grow; embedded products expand.
  • Cheaper, faster cover → better economics attract more agents and clients. Repeat.
