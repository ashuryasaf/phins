How these documents work. Open any document, choose its context / audience (investor, investment, employee, company, co-founder, counterparty) and edit the fields that matter — investor details, investment amount, salary, equity, vesting period, valuation, and more. Every relevant party gets a live signature panel; on signing, the signature, signer, and date & time are locked, the document content is hashed (SHA-256) and the signature is anchored append-only into PHINS's tamper-evident platform event ledger. Editing a signed document is blocked and any change is flagged. These are negotiation templates, not legal advice — have counsel review before use.
