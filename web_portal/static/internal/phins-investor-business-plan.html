This plan accompanies the introduction to Mr. Meir Uzan as a prospective investor and active co-founder.

2. Problem

  • Severe-disability and long-term-care (LTC) cover is under-served and hard to buy: legacy carriers run manual underwriting, fragmented data, and slow claims.
  • Ageing populations and rising disability prevalence increase demand faster than incumbents can serve it.
  • Buyers distrust opaque claims decisions; carriers struggle with fraud and severity validation.
  • Israel specifically has high private LTC interest and a home-care orientation, but no clean digital-first severity product.

3. Solution

PHINS is an AI-native insurance platform purpose-built for severe disability and LTC. It unifies underwriting, claims adjudication, billing, reinsurance, care navigation, and analytics in one digital-first operating system, with a transparent, auditable ledger so every balance and decision is reproducible.

  • AI underwriting — automated risk scoring from structured and unstructured medical data.
  • Claims intelligence — fraud / severity validation with multi-year look-back rules.
  • Objective severity trigger — permanent 3+ ADL standard keeps the claims promise tight and reinsurable.
  • Data integrity — append-only, hash-chained ledger; AI / BI may recommend but never post.

5. Business model

PHINS operates as a digital MGA / platform, earning a blended 25% take rate (MGA commission + technology fee) on gross written premium. Underwriting reserves and claims sit with the risk carrier / reinsurer, so they are not booked as PHINS revenue — this keeps the model conservative and capital-light. Additional revenue lines: SaaS platform fees, data services, and a PHINS-for-Actuaries B2B vertical.
