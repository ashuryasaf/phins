1.Platform objectives

PHINS is founder-led by Asaf Ashury, Adv. & Insurance Broker. The platform is already live at www.phins.ai. The investment case is the funded conversion of a built operating system into a regulated Israel launch — and then a repeatable multi-market deployment.

Objective 1

One insurance operating system

Underwrite, adjudicate, bill, reinsure, and prove every figure on a single AI-enhanced platform with an append-only, hash-chained ledger.

Objective 2

Israel launch, 1 January 2027

Close the build phase (legal terms, platform, QA, regulatory, funding) and begin sales only after the commercial-launch gate.

Objective 3

Capital-light digital MGA

Earn a blended 25% take on earned premium. Claims and reserves sit with the risk carrier — PHINS books platform economics, not underwriting risk.

Objective 4

Replicable multi-market path

Develop once; deploy across Israel first, then the USA, Canada, Europe and further regulated markets on the same integrity contract.

2.The need

  • Severe-disability and long-term-care cover is under-served and hard to buy: manual underwriting, fragmented data, and slow claims.
  • Ageing populations increase demand faster than incumbents can serve it.
  • Buyers distrust opaque claims; carriers struggle with fraud and severity validation.
  • Israel has high private LTC interest and a home-care orientation, but no clean digital-first severity product.

3.Solution

PHINS is an AI-enhanced insurance platform for severe disability and LTC. It unifies underwriting, claims, billing, reinsurance, care navigation, and analytics — with a transparent ledger so every balance is reproducible.

  • AI underwriting — risk scoring from structured and unstructured medical data.
  • Claims intelligence — fraud / severity validation with multi-year look-back.
  • Objective severity trigger — permanent 3+ ADL standard keeps the promise reinsurable.
  • Data integrity — append-only, hash-chained ledger; AI / BI may recommend but never post.

5.Business model

Digital MGA / platform: 25% blended take on earned GWP. Underwriting reserves and claims sit with the carrier / reinsurer and are not PHINS revenue. Additional lines: SaaS platform fees, data services, and a B2B actuarial vertical.
