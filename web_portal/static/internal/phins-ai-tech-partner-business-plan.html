🎯 Executive pitch — 3 minutes

What PHINS is. A production insurance platform — not a deck. Policy admin, AI underwriting, billing, claims adjudication, reinsurance structuring, wallets and settlement, agent commissions, and BI — live today, with every balance reproducible from append-only, hash-chained state.

Why an AI public company should care. Insurance is the largest regulated data industry still running on legacy cores. PHINS gives your AI capability a governed place to operate: the platform's integrity contract (AI recommends, the ledger decides) is precisely the architecture regulators are converging on. Co-developing PHINS puts your models inside underwriting, claims triage, and actuarial simulation — with an audit trail your compliance team can sign.

The market opening. Israel is the proof market (five-million-plus private LTC policyholders, home-care-first culture). The same codebase then opens OECD markets — each new market is a configuration and a filing, not a rebuild. Your public-market footprint and capital accelerate that sequence.

1. Company & founder

PHINS (Personal Health Insurance & Savings) was founded by Asaf Ashury, Adv. & Insurance Broker, who has developed the platform end-to-end: an AI-enhanced insurance stack purpose-built for disability and long-term-care severity products. The company operates a digital MGA / platform model — it earns a take rate on premium while underwriting risk sits with licensed carriers and reinsurers.

  • Built: 64+ deployed service modules — policy, underwriting, billing, claims, reinsurance, wallet/settlement, agent ecosystem, BI/actuarial.
  • Validated: an IAA-recognised executive actuary reviewed the pricing model (26 May 2026) and is joining on the hybrid Chief-Actuary path.
  • Governed: every financial event is anchored to an append-only, hash-chained platform ledger; AI layers recommend but never post.

2. The need

Disability and long-term-care exposure is the largest under-served personal risk in every OECD market, and the incumbent stacks cannot price or service it digitally. Carriers want the product but not the build; AI vendors have the models but no regulated surface to run them on. PHINS closes both gaps at once.

5. Business model

  • Core (IL) model: PHINS earns a blended 25% take rate on gross written premium as a digital MGA / platform. Claims reserves sit with the risk carrier and are never booked as PHINS revenue.
  • Partner-market model: per-market platform license fee plus a royalty on partner-market GWP — both adjustable in §7; shared between PHINS and the partner per the co-development agreement.
  • Equity track: strategic investment at the seed terms participates in the whole platform, not only the partner markets.
