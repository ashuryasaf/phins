🎯 Executive pitch — 3 minutes

The product. A permanent 3+ ADL disability / LTC severity layer — objectively triggered, digitally underwritten, home-care-first in claims navigation. Israel already thinks in LTC terms; the product gap is a carrier that can service it digitally at agent scale.

What PHINS brings. A production platform (64+ modules, validated pricing model reviewed by an IAA-recognised executive actuary): agent onboarding and commissions, digital application, AI-assisted underwriting with human sign-off, billing, claims adjudication, and reinsurance structuring — every financial event anchored to an append-only, hash-chained ledger.

What the insurer brings. Risk capacity (your paper), the agent force, brand, and regulatory standing. Your agents get a modern selling surface; your actuaries keep pricing sign-off; your reserves stay where they belong — with the carrier.

The two structures. (1) MGA: PHINS operates as your MGA — fastest to launch, distribution economics split from the take rate; (2) Joint company: a NewCo owns the market P&L — equity split adjustable in §7, JV valuation derivable from the actuarial appraisal simulation in §8. 1. Company & founder

PHINS (Personal Health Insurance & Savings) was founded by Asaf Ashury, Adv. & Insurance Broker, who has developed the platform end-to-end: an AI-enhanced insurance stack purpose-built for disability and long-term-care severity products. PHINS operates a digital MGA / platform model — it earns a take rate on premium while underwriting risk sits with licensed carriers and reinsurers.

  • Built: 64+ deployed service modules — policy, underwriting, billing, claims, reinsurance, wallet/settlement, agent ecosystem, BI/actuarial.
  • Validated: an IAA-recognised executive actuary reviewed the pricing model (26 May 2026) and is joining on the hybrid Chief-Actuary path.
  • Agent-native: the agent ecosystem (invitations, affiliations, commissions, ledger-anchored statements) is a deployed module, not a roadmap item.

2. The need

Israel has more than five million private LTC policyholders and a home-care-first claims culture — yet no carrier can originate and service a severity product digitally at agent scale. Agents lose sales to paperwork; carriers lose the product line to complexity. A joint build closes the gap in one launch.
