Executive Summary
Company Overview & Platform Architecture

PHINS (Personal Health Insurance & Savings) is a next-generation, AI-driven insurance and financial wellness platform purpose-built for the Spain market. PHINS unifies actuarially-sound disability and health insurance with a structured savings engine, an embedded health wallet, and algorithmic investment capabilities — all governed under a single regulatory framework compliant with the standards of the Dirección General de Seguros y Fondos de Pensiones and Comisión Nacional del Mercado de Valores (DGSFP / CNMV). Our technology stack replaces legacy manual processes with automated underwriting, AI fraud detection, predictive risk analytics, and real-time billing — reducing operational costs by up to 73% while delivering superior customer outcomes. PHINS targets individual and group policies with coverage starting at $50,000, priced transparently at €13.20/month for age 35 medium-risk customers on a 20-year term.

Core Service Offerings

Insurance Engine

ADL-based disability coverage with actuarial pricing. Base rate $0.25/$1K/month, age-adjusted, risk-stratified across 7 tiers. Compliant underwriting workflow with AI referral and approval automation.

Premium allocation with 50% savings split. Algorithmic trading targeting 9.2% annual returns. 20-year projection: €12,100+ accumulated savings on $50K policy for age-35 customer.

AI Automation Focus
End-to-End Automation Across Underwriting, Claims, and Operations
AI Module Function Regulatory Impact
🤖 Auto-Underwriting Instant policy approval/referral/rejection based on ADL questionnaire, age, risk tier, and medical history flags. Decision in <60 seconds. Eliminates subjective underwriter bias; creates full decision audit trail for regulatory review.
🔍 Claims Fraud Detection ML model scores every claim submission. Flags anomalous patterns: duplicate claims, temporal clustering, provider fraud rings. 94%+ fraud detection rate; reduces claims leakage; supports AML compliance.
📊 AI Risk Reports Automated risk stratification reports per customer cohort, policy type, and geographic segment. Generated weekly. Replaces manual actuarial reporting; provides regulator-ready data exports.
💬 AI Customer Assistant 24/7 chatbot handles policy queries, coverage explanations, claim status, payment confirmations. Escalates to human agent on complex cases. Meets 24hr complaint acknowledgement SLA; reduces call centre costs by 62%.
📈 Predictive Analytics Churn prediction, lapse probability, upsell triggers, and cohort lifetime value scoring updated monthly. Enables proactive customer retention; supports solvency reserve planning.
💳 Auto-Billing Automated premium collection, partial payment reconciliation, late fee application, and billing statement generation. Zero manual touchpoints for standard policies. Full billing audit trail; prevents revenue leakage; supports AML transaction monitoring.
