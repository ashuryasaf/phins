ADL Disability Coverage — Product Architecture and Actuarial Model Overview
Flagship Product: ADL Disability Coverage

The PHINS flagship product is an Activities of Daily Living (ADL) long-term disability insurance policy. ADL coverage pays a lump-sum benefit when the insured is unable to perform a defined number of ADL activities (eating, dressing, bathing, mobility, continence, and transferring), providing financial security for individuals and families.

7. Claims Reserve Projections
Reserve Adequacy Analysis — IBNR, Catastrophe Reserve, and Coverage Ratios (₪ Millions)
Reserve Policy: PHINS maintains actuarially certified claims reserves consistent with the Israeli Commissioner of Insurance's capital adequacy framework. Reserves include: (a) Incurred But Not Reported (IBNR) at 25% of expected annual claims; (b) Catastrophe reserve for tail events; and (c) target reserve coverage ratio ≥150%.

Reserve Methodology
📊

Expected Claims

Calculated using actuarially certified ADL disability incidence tables, policy mix, and in-force exposure. Grows proportional to policy count.

🔍

IBNR Reserve

Incurred But Not Reported reserve set at 25% of expected annual claims. Covers claims with reporting lags typical in disability insurance.

🌪️

Catastrophe Reserve

Maintained for tail risks including systemic health events, pandemic scenarios, and mass casualty events. Set conservatively at ₪0.10–₪0.50M.

Reserve Adequacy: The reserve coverage ratio declines from 183% (Year 1) to 153% (Year 3) as the portfolio scales. All coverage ratios exceed the minimum 150% target and the regulatory minimum. The actuarial reserve is reviewed annually by an independent qualified actuary. Results are included in annual statutory filings with the Commissioner of Insurance and ISA.

✅ Regulatory Compliance

All reserve calculations comply with the Israeli Insurance Business (Control) Law, 5741-1981, the Commissioner of Insurance Circular on Reserve Adequacy, and IFRS 17 (Insurance Contracts) which is applicable to TASE-listed insurance companies.

Section 10 — Risk Factors
10. Risk Factors
Key risks that prospective investors should consider before investing in PHINS shares

Investment in securities involves risk. The following risk factors are not exhaustive. Investors should read this section carefully and consult their own advisers before making any investment decision.

🏢 Regulatory Risk

PHINS operates in a heavily regulated sector. Changes to the Israeli Securities Law, Insurance Business (Control) Law, or Commissioner of Insurance guidelines may adversely affect operations, capital requirements, or product offerings.

📉 Actuarial Risk

If actual ADL disability claims exceed actuarial projections, reserve adequacy may be impaired. Adverse claims development could reduce profitability and require additional capital injections.

💱 Currency Risk

Premiums are calculated in USD and converted to ILS at 3.68. Significant USD/ILS exchange rate movements may affect revenue recognition, pricing competitiveness, and reserve calculations.

🤖 Technology Risk

The business model is highly dependent on AI underwriting and automation systems. System failures, cybersecurity breaches, or AI model degradation could disrupt operations and damage customer trust.

🏦 Capital & Liquidity Risk

Year 1 projects a net loss of ₪0.41M. If IPO proceeds are insufficient or customer acquisition is slower than projected, additional capital may be required. There is no guarantee of profitability.

🌐 Market Penetration Risk

Achieving the 1,200–12,000 active wallet customer targets requires successful marketing in a competitive Israeli insurance market. Competition from established insurers (Harel, Migdal, Phoenix, Clal) may limit growth.

📋 Compliance & Reporting Risk

TASE-listed companies are subject to ongoing continuous disclosure obligations, quarterly reporting, audit requirements, and corporate governance standards. Failure to comply may result in regulatory sanctions.

🌡️ Catastrophe Risk

A widespread health catastrophe (pandemic, mass casualty event) could trigger simultaneous ADL disability claims across the insured portfolio, exceeding catastrophe reserves. Reinsurance arrangements will be implemented to mitigate this risk.
