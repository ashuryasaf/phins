Underwriting Process & Requirements

Our underwriting process ensures fair and accurate assessment while providing transparent criteria for coverage approval.

📋

Application Requirements

  • ✓ Personal Information: Full legal name, date of birth, contact details
  • ✓ Medical History: Comprehensive health questionnaire
  • ✓ Lifestyle Assessment: Occupation, activities, habits
  • ✓ Financial Documentation: Income verification for coverage amount
  • ✓ Identification: Government-issued ID and proof of address
🏥

Medical Requirements

  • ✓ Health Questionnaire: Detailed medical history form
  • ✓ Medical Examination: Required for coverage over $1M
  • ✓ Lab Tests: Blood work, urinalysis (coverage dependent)
  • ✓ Medical Records: Release for existing condition review
  • ✓ Specialist Reports: If pre-existing conditions present
⚖️

Risk Assessment Criteria

  • ✓ Age: Coverage available ages 18-65
  • ✓ Health Status: Current and historical conditions
  • ✓ Occupation: Risk level assessment based on job
  • ✓ Lifestyle Factors: Exercise, smoking, alcohol use
  • ✓ Family History: Hereditary condition considerations
⏱️

Processing Timeline

  • Day 1-2: Initial application review and completeness check
  • Day 3-7: Medical examination scheduling and completion
  • Day 8-14: Medical records and lab results review
  • Day 15-21: Final underwriting decision and premium calculation
  • Day 22+: Policy issuance and activation

Understanding Severe Disability & ADLs

Activities of Daily Living (ADLs) are the fundamental self-care tasks that define functional independence:

  • 1. Bathing: Washing oneself and maintaining personal hygiene
  • 2. Dressing: Putting on and removing clothing independently
  • 3. Eating: Feeding oneself (not meal preparation)
  • 4. Toileting: Using the toilet and maintaining continence
  • 5. Transferring: Moving in/out of bed, chair, wheelchair
  • 6. Continence: Controlling bladder and bowel functions

PHINS Coverage Trigger: Benefits activate when you are permanently unable to perform 3 or more ADLs without substantial assistance. This represents severe functional impairment requiring comprehensive long-term care - the exact gap most insurance policies fail to cover adequately.
