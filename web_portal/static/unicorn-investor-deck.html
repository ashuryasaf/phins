The world is getting older and living longer, but the systems that protect people against severe disability and the cost of long-term care are analog, adversarial, and underfunded. PHINS exists to make protection a software product: priced by AI, paid by code, and orchestrated across everyone who delivers care.

02 · Problem

A trillion-dollar protection gap, served by software built before the smartphone.

Why people are unprotected

  • Aging + longevity shock. Populations are aging fast while public long-term-care budgets strain — the private gap widens every year.
  • Severe disability is mispriced or excluded. Generic disability wording is hard to price, so carriers retreat or overcharge.
  • Claims are adversarial. The moment of need is the moment of friction — slow, manual, opaque adjudication.
  • Agents are stuck in software from the 1990s. Quoting, compliance and servicing are manual, fragmenting the customer relationship.

Why incumbents can't fix it

  • Legacy core systems bolt AI onto decades-old infrastructure; data is trapped and unauditable.
  • Channel conflict — carriers fear disintermediating their own agents, so they under-invest in agent software.
  • Capital intensity — balance-sheet carriers can't move at software speed or margin.
  • No data flywheel — underwriting, claims and care data sit in separate silos and never compound.
The protection gap is not a demand problem. It is a software, pricing and trust problem — exactly the kind that an AI-native platform with flawless data integrity is built to solve.

06 · AI Architecture

Two AI moats: underwriting intelligence and claims intelligence — both auditable.

🧠 AI underwriting moat

  • Per-segment thresholds tuned to severity (permanent 3+ ADL), not generic morbidity.
  • Model registry versions every model; decisions are explainable and replayable.
  • Proprietary data from each bound policy sharpens pricing competitors can't see.
  • Reinsurance-ready artifacts — objective adjudication evidence improves treaty terms.

⚙️ AI claims intelligence moat

  • Triage & routing shorten non-permanent claim pathways and reduce false positives.
  • Code-based settlement of eligible benefits — fast, consistent, anchored to the ledger.
  • Care navigation routes home-care first, improving loss economics and outcomes.
  • Fraud & integrity checks run against tamper-evident, hash-chained records.
Governance: AI and BI layers are advisory by contract — they recommend, score and explain, but financial postings are made only through ledger-governed transactions. This is what lets us promise flawless data integrity to regulators, reinsurers and investors simultaneously.

08 · Marketplace Model

A protection marketplace connecting clients, agents, doctors, reinsurers & care providers.

Five-sided network

  • Clients get protection + a path to care.
  • Agents get AgentOS + a marketplace of products.
  • Doctors / care providers get verified demand & ledger-clean settlement.
  • Reinsurers get clean, objective risk & better treaty data.
  • Payers get recovery, remittance & auditable claims.

How it monetizes

  • Take rate on marketplace GMV (care, services, embedded products).
  • Wallet & settlement — holds, settlement runs, payer recovery, remittances.
  • Data & BI products for carriers and reinsurers (governed, privacy-safe).
Marketplace + AgentOS create network effects: more agents → more clients → more care demand → better provider supply & reinsurance terms → cheaper, faster protection → more agents.
