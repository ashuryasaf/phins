What the customer holds on a US$150,000 cover
The same locked risk tariff as the Fefferman brief, bought at a US$150,000 face, plus the rights that sit beside the cover: the service of the policy, the savings add-on, the health wallet, and consumption in the health marketplace. Every figure below is computed from one source block.

PLANNING ILLUSTRATION. This page states the canonical contract for one reference customer. It is a planning illustration for an investment reader. The risk tariff uses the published adjustable-risk identity (life base US$0.25 and disability base US$0.20 per US$1,000 per month, age factor 1.000 at age 25, slope +0.015 a year). The savings add-on is half of that tariff. Wallet and savings balances are the contributions themselves: yield 0 and management fee 0, the kernel default. A missing incidence rate is left blank and is never shown as zero.

2Rights

  • Service. Once underwriting is approved and the risk tariff is paid, the life and disability sums above stay in force. Re-pricing follows the published age curve only. The customer can read the decision, the age band and the formula, withdraw within 30 days for a pro-rata refund of unearned tariff, and follow every charge on the ledger.
  • Savings. The customer elects an add-on equal to half the risk tariff. That amount is the customer's savings. Of it, 65% is credited to the long-term savings sleeve and 5% to the algo sleeve.
  • Health wallet. 30% of each savings add-on is credited to the health wallet. The balance is the customer's, visible on the ledger.
  • Consumption. The customer spends the health-wallet balance in the marketplace on ranked care, medicine and equipment. In this illustration consumption is US$0, so the wallet balance is the amount available to consume.

3Liabilities

  • Service. The age-adjusted risk tariff is payable to keep both benefits, including after 65 on the stepped-down sums. Material health, work and lifestyle facts are disclosed at underwriting. A disability event is notified within 60 days and a death within 30 days, with the medical record.
  • Savings. The add-on is payable with the tariff for as long as it stays elected. It is the customer's contribution. It is outside the carrier risk fund.
  • Health wallet. Credits follow the 30% rule. A withdrawal or a transfer moves that balance and is recorded.
  • Consumption. A marketplace purchase is paid from the wallet. The life sum and the disability sum stay at the contracted amounts. A purchase larger than the balance is declined.
