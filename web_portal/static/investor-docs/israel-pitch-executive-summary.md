# PHINS — Israel (IL) Pitch: Executive Summary

**Audience:** Investors evaluating PHINS's Israel launch market.
**Companion:** Live, adjustable figures are in the PHINS Scenario Lab on the
pitch dashboard (`/pitch-dashboard.html`). This summary is intentionally
qualitative; all quantitative TAM, ceded-premium, and margin figures should be
read from the live model, where public data is locked and PHINS assumptions are
adjustable.

---

## Why Israel first

Israel is the strongest first narrative for a digital-first severity product:

- **High home-care orientation.** Care delivery skews toward home and community
  settings, which fits a digital-first, severity-triggered benefit and
  marketplace orchestration layer.
- **Large private long-term-care (LTC) penetration.** A substantial private LTC
  in-force base demonstrates established willingness to pay for protection
  beyond the public system.
- **Visible public spend.** A clear public expenditure baseline makes the
  protection gap legible to regulators, partners, and customers.

Together these create a credible launch market where PHINS can enter as a
top-up and orchestration layer rather than as a public-system replacement.

## The product thesis

PHINS should sell a **severity layer**, not a generic disability promise:

- An objectively severe, permanent **3+ ADL** trigger keeps the product
  underwritable and reinsurable.
- The health wallet is the checkout and reimbursement center; suppliers are
  controlled, network-governed participants.
- Coverage-aware ranking (not cheapest-only) considers coverage fit, SLA,
  clinical suitability, and fraud risk.

## Regulatory & actuarial posture

- Israel is one of three candidate first-filing jurisdictions (IL · CA · US).
  PHINS's actuarial stack (mortality/disability tables, lapse, IFRS-17-style
  projection, IBNR, and integrity-hashed valuations) supports an Appointed
  Actuary review and investor diligence.
- The platform's data-integrity backbone — append-only, hash-chained ledger with
  reconcilable wallet, settlement, and reserve views — is designed so every
  balance is reproducible from postings.

## Data integrity (why the numbers can be trusted)

This pitch is produced on a platform whose core invariant is that **every
balance and decision is reproducible from append-only, hash-chained state, and
derived AI/BI layers may recommend but never post**. Public evidence and PHINS
scenario assumptions are kept separate by design, so the model can be stress-
tested without corrupting the underlying statistics.
