## Elevator pitch — 45 seconds

Long-term cover is disappearing while national insurance schemes face
demographic strain. PHINS is an **AI-operated insurance operating system
already in production**, on a hash-chained ledger where **AI recommends
but never posts**. Separately — and only second — its first product is
the **PHINS Unified Insurance Contract**: pure-risk life with a lifelong
3+ ADL disability mechanism, priced on a deterministic actuarial
simulator and filed tables. Israel first; OECD adapters on the same
codebase. Today's conversation is readiness, markets, and a scale
identity of **up to 250,000 issued policies by end-2029**. Valuation and
any ask stay off the table.
