PHINS strength for this point of view

Counsel is not being asked to invent the company on paper.

  • One book. Premium, in-force, take rate, seed, and use of funds regenerate from one factor register. A scenario on the live plan does not rewrite this brief.
  • The record is the control. Recommendations carry evidence. The ledger is append-only and hash-chained. That is the sentence a regulator and a tax authority can both test.
  • Identity is already vaulted. A customer's national ID has one writer. Responses carry nationality, a hash, and the last four digits. Counsel should not design a second store.
  • Documents already share one chrome. Navy and gold, the shield, Inter and Space Grotesk. Legal markup should change words, not the figures and not the letterhead.
  • The round is small and fully allocated. ILS 2,400,000 technology, ILS 1,200,000 regulatory / actuarial / reinsurance, ILS 1,500,000 distribution and local partnerships, ILS 900,000 operations and reserve. Those four lines sum to ILS 6,000,000.
