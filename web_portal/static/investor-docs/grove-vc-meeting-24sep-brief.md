## 2 · Technology — deep tech for insurance and finance

The saving is not a thinner call centre. It is a different control system
for a regulated money flow.

| Function | What the technology does | Planning automation |
|---|---|---|
| Underwriting | Severity models decide in minutes; a person sees only flagged edge cases | ~92% |
| Billing | Invoicing, auto-pay, and dunning post on the ledger | ~96% |
| Claims | AI triage and fraud signals; a person adjudicates complex or contested claims | ~88% |
| Distribution | Digital-first onboarding plus a tooled agent ecosystem | Digital-first |
| Record | Hash-chained ledger; every recommendation is auditable | AI recommends, ledger decides |

That is the deep-tech fit. Insurance and finance fail long-duration cover
when human touch is priced into every policy. PHINS keeps human judgment
where it changes the outcome — edge underwriting, contested claims,
governance — and automates the rest. A new market is a regulatory adapter
and a filing, not a second codebase. Expense ratio is planned to improve
about 1.5 percentage points a year through that automation (Scenario Lab;
adjustable; not an actuarial projection).
