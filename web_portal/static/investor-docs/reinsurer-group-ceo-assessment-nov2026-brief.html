PHINS — Global Reinsurer Group CEO Assessment (7 November 2026)

Internal · Confidential. A planning exercise written from the seat of the Group CEO of a global reinsurance group, answering a regional CEO who has escalated PHINS as a strategic opportunity. It sets the measures of risk appetite, and of InsurTech openness and venture exposure, that such a group would apply to long-term disability, long-term care and savings solutions, and it assesses PHINS against them with every option kept on the table. No reinsurer has been approached, none is named as a counterparty, and nothing here is a term. The only counterparty on a treaty is the licensed carrier of record; PHINS is the technology and the administrator.

Decision in one paragraph. Keep all options on the table. The region may sign the NDA, open the data room and run technical due diligence on the carrier-of-record structure and the pricing kernel; it may give a non-binding indication on the quota share the plan already assumes (18% cession, 25% ceding commission) with the experience-rating features set out below; it may not bind capacity before the Israeli filing is accepted and the carrier of record is named, and it may not link a venture ticket to a cession. A venture participation, if any, sits inside the ILS 6,000,000 seed on ILS 24,000,000 pre-money — there is no reinsurer round and no side price. Group Risk sets the limits; this memo sets the measures.

What the regional CEO has put on the table

  • The contract. Life insurance with a lifelong severe-disability mechanism: face ILS 1,000,000, disability ILS 250,000 below 65 (1:4), 1:1 on the reduced sum from 65, trigger permanent loss of three or more activities of daily living. Price at the age-42 specimen ILS 4,518 a year (ILS 376.50 a month: 313.75 life + 62.75 disability, age factor 1.255, published tables 0.25 / 0.20 per ILS 1,000 monthly). Draft 3.1. Not approved for sale. Pre-ruling track opened 12 October 2026; sales from 1 January 2027 only after Gate 5.
  • The structure. Carried by a licensed insurer, reinsured by quota share from day one, distributed with a local partner; PHINS is the technology at a 25% take, the carrier keeps 75%. The savings add-on (30% election × 3.0× at default) is a separately licensed flow, never split with the carrier or the reinsurer.
  • The book. Israel in-force 24,000 / 94,080 / 230,554 (2027 / 2028 / 2029) and 471,621 by 2031; risk GWP ₪54.2M → ₪733.3M → ₪1.87B; PHINS net ILS 13,554,000 / 66,685,680 / 183,337,052. Ten markets on one platform by 2031; group risk GWP ₪3.75B ($1.02B at the planning FX of 3.68); group PHINS revenue ₪749.1M ($203.6M) at a 57% EBITDA margin.
  • The round. Seed ILS 6,000,000 on ILS 24,000,000 pre-money (ILS 30,000,000 post, 20%). 2027 burn ₪18.8M; peak cumulative need ₪20.2M; Series A indication ₪18.0M (2028). Actuarial appraisal ₪264.6M (35% WACC, 4.0× 2029 net revenue, 15% prudence); the ask is 9% of it. Use of seed: ILS 1,200,000 (20%) for regulatory, actuarial and reinsurance.
  • The reinsurer's line in their own plan is a placeholder. Expected claims 62%, cession 18%, ceding commission 25%, carrier own expenses 5% are factor-register defaults "to be replaced by the carrier's and the reinsurer's term sheets" (Appendix I). That is the right posture for a counterparty to meet.

InsurTech openness and venture exposure — the measures

  • Three rails, never linked. Capacity (treaty), venture (equity) and services (carrier introductions, placement, data) are priced on their own. No cession is priced for an equity ticket and no equity is priced for a cession. A linked price is the measure of a bad deal.
  • Venture exposure is bounded by the round. Seed ILS 6,000,000 on ILS 24,000,000 pre-money; a participation is inside that seed at the same price as every other investor, with no reinsurer round, no side price and no control sought at seed. The Series A indication of ₪18.0M (2028) is the second entry point and is decided at Gate 7 on data, not on the pitch. The measures are the ticket as a share of the venture sleeve (Group), ownership kept below the level that would make PHINS an affiliate for cession purposes (Group), and information rights: data room, decision-log access, quarterly experience against the 62% planning ratio.
  • Openness, read from the reported record (Techno Yes registry on the pitch dashboard, each with its source label): Munich Re / ERGO acquired NEXT Insurance for US$2.6B (1 July 2025) and At-Bay for US$575M through HSB (August 2026); Fortitude Re took Dayforward's technology platform (September 2026) with the insurance entities excluded; Hannover Re funds Lemonade's growth with a US$250M facility; Munich Re reinsures Ethos (IPO 30 January 2026, 95% of applications instantly underwritten); Swiss Re wound down iptiQ, its own B2B2C digital carrier. The pattern: reinsurers partner, fund and buy the MGA; they do not run the retail brand. PHINS as the technology and MGA layer on a carrier's paper fits the pattern; PHINS as a reinsurer subsidiary would not.
  • The acquirable unit, if ever, is the administered book with its telemetry. The hash-chained ledger, evidence provenance on every fact and the one-writer identity master are the analogue of the telemetry a reinsurer paid for at At-Bay. An option on that is bought with the information rights above, not with capacity.

Delegation to the region — what the regional CEO may do at his discretion

  • Without Group. Sign the NDA; open the data room; run technical due diligence (kernel, ledger, decision log, golden sets, identity contract); attend the Appointed Actuary review at Gate 6; give a non-binding capacity indication on the plan's own 18% / 25% defaults with the experience-rating features named above; introduce client carriers in the region.
  • With Group Risk. Bind any capacity; set the cession, commission, corridor and aggregate sum-at-risk limits; approve any multi-market wording.
  • With Group (venture committee). Any equity ticket, at seed or Series A; any board or observer seat; any exclusivity.
  • Never. A linked price; a reinsurer-run retail brand; capacity before the filing is accepted; any commitment on the savings flow.

Conditions under which we would pass

  • The filing is not accepted, or a jurisdiction requires a different carrier structure and it is not cured.
  • The carrier of record is not named, or will not grant audit rights to the decision log and to bordereaux reconciled to the ledger.
  • The experience at Gate 6 (first 1,000 policies) or Gate 7 (24,000 in force) is not reviewed by an Appointed Actuary against the kernel.
  • A linked price is requested.

Due diligence asks on PHINS — the regional team's checklist

  • Pricing. services/pricing_kernel.py as the one writer; the published risk_reference_v1 tables; the integrity hash on every quote; the repricing path through the one writer.
  • Governance. ai_decision_log entries for underwriting and claims recommendations with rule score, model score and divergence; audited threshold promotion with before and after snapshots; golden sets gating CI.
  • Integrity. The hash-chained platform ledger; the plan's content hash; the Appendix E identities, all holding.
  • Identity and privacy. One writer for the national ID; records carry hash and last-4 only; the number never returned.
  • Regulatory. The pre-ruling record of 12 October 2026; Draft 3.1; the Gate 5 conditions; the regulatory pathway per partner market (Appendix C).
  • Capital. Seed close; 2027 burn ₪18.8M; Series A ₪18.0M; use of seed with ILS 1,200,000 (20%) for regulatory, actuarial and reinsurance.
