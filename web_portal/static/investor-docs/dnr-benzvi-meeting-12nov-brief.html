Purpose. Show an agency CEO a product his agents can explain, a platform that tools the agent instead of replacing the house, and a canonical seed if he wants to look at the company as an investor. Do not offer a private commission rate. None has been set.

Point of view — agency distribution, then capital

  • Who he is. Yosi Ben Zvi has spent decades in Israeli insurance, previously as an owner of Prisma, and he runs D.N.R. as a private house whose public stance is that an agent should cover the whole client. That is the sentence to respect: PHINS is one contract family (life plus lifelong severe disability), not a fragment the agent has to apologise for.
  • What he is not. He is not the licensed carrier. The 75% insurance take stays with a designated insurer. D.N.R. would distribute.
  • Local only, as the lead. The book to walk is Israel: premium ILS 4,518, in-force 24,000 / 94,080 / 230,554, PHINS net ILS 13,554,000 / 66,685,680 / 183,337,052. Sales from 1 January 2027, and only after Gate 5.
  • The agency channel is already in the model. Distribution is digital-first, with a tooled agent ecosystem: invitations, affiliations, commissions, and an agent ledger. A house of agents is a collaboration channel on that rail. It is not a second platform.
  • Investment is the same seed. If he looks at capital, the round is ILS 6,000,000 on ILS 24,000,000 pre-money. The distribution line inside that round is ILS 1,500,000. There is no separate "agency round".

PHINS strength for this point of view

  • An agent can say the product in one breath. Pure-risk life, face ILS 1,000,000, disability ILS 250,000 below age 65, trigger is permanent loss of three or more activities of daily living, price at the age-42 specimen is ILS 4,518 a year. From 65 the mechanism is 1:1 on the reduced sum. Draft 3.1. Not yet approved for sale.
  • The house keeps its role. PHINS does not ask D.N.R. to become a carrier, a reinsurer, or a software company. The platform does intake, underwriting support, billing, and claims triage. The agent keeps the client relationship. Planning benchmarks: underwriting about 92%, billing about 96%, claims about 88%. A person still decides the edge case.
  • Commission loading is designed down, not denied. The 25% PHINS take has to pay for distribution as well as operations. Do not quote a D.N.R. commission. Say that any house agreement is a commercial document still to be written, on top of the carrier's paper, and that NBA-style counsel or the carrier's counsel will paper it. Do not invent the split in the room.
  • The record protects the agent. Every recommendation has evidence. The ledger is hash-chained. A complaint or a regulator question has a file, not a recollection.
  • Integrity of the offer. One plan, one price, one product. An agency investor should see the same book a financial investor sees.
