## Functional MGA — who holds what

The structure we ask the Authority to pre-rule: PHINS runs the operating
functions; the designated Israeli insurance company carries the risk; an
investment company carries the savings add-on risk (counterparties TBA).

| Function | PHINS (functional MGA) | Counterparty |
|---|---|---|
| Underwriting | AI severity underwriting within carrier-approved guidelines; humans on flagged edge cases. | Designated Israeli insurance company approves guidelines and holds the risk. |
| Billing | Automated billing, auto-pay and dunning on the platform ledger. | Premium flows reconciled to the carrier; savings component to the investment company (TBA). |
| Claims | AI triage + fraud detection; human adjudication on complex or contested claims. | Claims reserves stay on the carrier; carrier retains final adjudication authority. |
| Actuary | Adjustable pricing / reserving models; every assumption change versioned and ledger-logged. | Carrier's appointed actuary signs off; Authority has audit visibility. |
| Savings add-on | Platform administration and unified customer view. | Investment company (TBA) carries the savings add-on risk under its own licence. |

**So what:** PHINS is the managerial and operational layer — **not** the risk
carrier — so the pre-ruling can approve the platform posture without
transferring insurance risk to an unlicensed entity; the future
insurance-company option is a separate, staged decision.
