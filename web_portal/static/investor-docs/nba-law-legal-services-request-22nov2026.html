About PHINS

PHINS is an insurance technology company building an insurance operating system for the Israeli market: one platform for pricing, underwriting, billing, claims, and records, with an append-only, hash-chained audit trail. Automated tools recommend; people decide.

  • Product. The first contract is life insurance with a lifelong disability mechanism (permanent loss of three or more activities of daily living). The wording is in actuarial draft (Draft 3.1) and is not yet approved.
  • Model. PHINS intends to operate as a digital managing general agent (MGA) on the paper of a designated licensed Israeli insurer, with reinsurance behind that insurer. PHINS is not a licensed insurer. The planned economics are 25% of risk premium to PHINS and 75% to the insurer; no insurer agreement is signed.
  • Regulator. A pre-ruling track with the Capital Market, Insurance and Savings Authority opened on 12 October 2026. Sales are planned from 1 January 2027, subject to approval.
  • Capital. A proposed seed round of ILS 6,000,000 on a pre-money valuation of ILS 24,000,000 (ILS 30,000,000 post-money), with an indicative Series A of ILS 18,000,000 later. Neither round is closed.
  • Adjacent services on the platform. An optional savings add-on with an investment company, a customer health wallet, customer referral rewards, and an agent network.

1. Corporate and contracts

Services requested

  • Advise on the entity structure and seat: an Israeli company, or a Delaware parent with an Israeli subsidiary. PHINS's current drafts default to a Delaware C-Corporation and Delaware law, and its materials also refer to PHINS Technologies Ltd.; we ask the firm to recommend one structure.
  • Review and mark up the founder agreement, the co-founder exit clause, the cap table, and the shareholders agreement, including vesting and the path for an executive actuary to join as co-founder.
  • Advise on the seed instrument (priced round or convertible) at the proposed terms, the option pool, and investor rights that remain workable for a Series A.
  • Prepare or review investor and partner non-disclosure agreements, including electronically signed versions.
  • Review the customer terms of use (currently drafted under New York law) and the online application and claims flows for an Israeli consumer contract, including electronic signature and Hebrew versions.
  • Review the employment agreement, offer letter, option plan, and HR policies under Israeli labour law, and the contractor, agent, and supplier agreements.
  • Review the privacy notice and consent wording, and prepare data-processing agreements with cloud and AI service providers.

Deliverables: structure memorandum; marked-up founder, shareholder, and seed documents; Israeli customer terms and privacy notice; employment and contractor templates; NDA templates.

2. Intellectual property

Services requested

  • Confirm the ownership chain of the platform code and documents: assignments from founders, employees, and contractors, including service inventions and any compensation waiver.
  • Advise on ownership of code, documents, and media produced with the assistance of AI tools, and on the terms of the AI and video providers used.
  • Run or supervise an open-source licence review of third-party software before investor diligence.
  • Advise on protection of the policy wording, pricing method, underwriting process, and evidence pipeline: patent filing, copyright, or trade secret; and on ownership of wording filed with the regulator or shared with an insurer.
  • File and maintain the PHINS name and shield mark in Israel and prospective partner markets; domain names.
  • Prepare a licence template for partner markets (per-market licence and royalty) and the platform licence terms for the insurer.
  • Review the confidentiality and trade-secret practice: NDAs, access records, and signed-document evidence.

Deliverables: ownership-chain report and corrective assignments; IP protection memorandum; trademark filings; licence templates.

3. Tax

Services requested

  • Advise on the tax consequences of the entity structure, residence, transfer pricing, and the location of the intellectual property.
  • Advise on the seed and the indicative Series A, investor-side tax incentives, and founder shares issued before the round.
  • Advise on the tax and VAT treatment of the MGA income (25% of risk premium) and platform fees, and on any financial-institution status.
  • Set up employee equity under section 102 (trustee track) and align the option documents.
  • Advise on technology-enterprise incentives and Israel Innovation Authority funding, including any limits on moving IP abroad.
  • Advise on reporting and withholding for customer referral rewards, health-wallet withdrawals, and the savings add-on.
  • Advise on reinsurance premiums paid abroad and on entry into a partner market.

Deliverables: tax structure memorandum; section 102 plan and trustee set-up; positions on VAT, rewards, and cross-border payments; list of any rulings to request.

4. Israeli and segmented regulation

Services requested, segment by segment:

  • Insurance product. Support on the path from the 12 October 2026 pre-ruling track to an approved filing by the designated insurer.
  • MGA and distribution. The licences PHINS needs to underwrite, bill, and handle claims for an insurer; agent licensing; distribution through insurance agencies without transferring risk to them.
  • Referral rewards. Whether, and on what terms, customers who are not licensed agents may receive a reward for referring a new customer, and the line between such rewards and agent commissions.
  • Savings add-on. Licensing of the savings flow and the investment-company partner; investment advice and marketing rules; access to pension clearing-house (Mislaka) data.
  • Health wallet and payments. Payment-services and anti-money-laundering obligations on wallet balances, holds, and withdrawals.
  • Data and AI. The Privacy Protection Law and its data-security regulations for health and identity data; cross-border transfers to cloud and AI providers; disclosure of automated recommendations to customers.
  • Outsourcing and records. Insurer outsourcing requirements for a platform that performs underwriting, billing, and claims; regulator and insurer audit access; record retention.
  • Partner markets. What changes per market, including GDPR and the EU AI Act when an EU market opens.

Deliverables: regulatory map per segment with required licences, filings, and timing; filing support; compliance checklist for launch.

5. Insurance and reinsurance commercial

Services requested

  • Draft or negotiate the agreement with the designated insurer (MGA or binding authority): underwriting and claims authority and limits, the actuarial function, and the decisions reserved to the insurer.
  • Premium collection, trust or client-money accounts, remittance, and the fee and any profit-share terms.
  • Ownership of policyholder data and of the book on termination; portability; the platform licence to the insurer.
  • Service levels, audit and inspection rights, and regulator access to records.
  • Liability: errors-and-omissions insurance, caps, indemnities, and claims disputes.
  • Term, exclusivity, termination, run-off, and an option after year three to move the insurance share to a PHINS-group Israeli insurer.
  • Reinsurance terms with the insurer as cedent. The plan assumes an 18% quota share of risk premium with a 25% ceding commission (ILS 9,758,880 of ceded premium in 2027). Scope includes bordereaux, claims cooperation, profit commission, and lead-reinsurer terms.
  • Review of the NDA and any non-binding indication letter before the meeting with Swiss Re Israel on 8 December 2026.
  • The agreement with the investment company for the savings add-on, and agency distribution agreements.

Deliverables: insurer agreement term sheet and draft; reinsurance term sheet review; NDA and indication-letter review before 8 December 2026; distribution and savings partner agreements.

Engagement capacity requested

  • Team. One coordinating partner and a named partner for each of the five areas. Corporate, IP, tax, regulatory, and insurance commercial work proceed in parallel.
  • Conflicts. A conflict check covering the Capital Market, Insurance and Savings Authority, Swiss Re, D.N.R. Insurance Agency, the prospective designated insurers, and the prospective seed investors (names provided on request).
  • Fees. A fee proposal per area, with a fixed or capped fee for defined deliverables where possible and an hourly basis for open-ended work.
  • Sequence. First deliverables in this order: entity and tax structure; IP ownership chain; seed documents; the reinsurer papers before 8 December 2026; the regulatory map; the insurer agreement.
  • Working method. English and Hebrew documents; a fortnightly working call; markup of existing drafts rather than new drafting where a draft exists.
  • Confidentiality. All materials are shared under NDA.
