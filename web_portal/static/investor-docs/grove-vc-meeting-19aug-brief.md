## Elevator pitch — 30 seconds

The world is losing its long-term cover: private carriers are retreating from
disability / LTC lines and national insurance schemes face predicted collapse
globally. PHINS is an **AI-operated insurance operating system** already in
production that automates the human-touch layer — underwriting, billing,
claims, agents — so lifelong cover becomes affordable again, on a hash-chained
ledger where **AI recommends but never posts**. Founder-led, regulator pathway
documented, raising an **ILS 6.0M seed at ILS 24M pre-money**.

## The global problem — open with the need, not the product

- **Long-term covers are disappearing.** Private carriers worldwide are
  withdrawing from, re-pricing, or capping standalone long-term care and
  lifelong disability lines.
- **National insurance is under predicted collapse — globally.** Ageing
  populations and shrinking worker-to-beneficiary ratios put pay-as-you-go
  state disability / LTC schemes on projected deficit paths; benefit erosion
  is already visible.
- **The gap lands on households** exactly when demographic need peaks.
- **Why the market failed:** human-heavy operations (underwriting, billing,
  claims, agent distribution) make long-duration cover uneconomic to originate
  and administer at accessible premiums.
- **The PHINS thesis:** remove the human-cost layer with an AI-operated
  platform so long-term cover becomes affordable, sustainable, and globally
  scalable — Israel first, multi-market on one codebase.

## Technology vs human touch — where the savings come from

| Function | Traditional (human touch) | PHINS (AI-operated) | Automation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Underwriting | Manual underwriter review; days–weeks per case | AI severity models decide in minutes; humans only on flagged edge cases | ~92% |
| Billing | Manual invoicing, reconciliation, collections teams | Automated billing, auto-pay, and dunning on the platform ledger | ~96% |
| Claims | Adjuster-led handling; loss-adjustment expense on every claim | AI triage + fraud detection; human adjudication only for complex or contested claims | ~88% |
| Agents / distribution | Commission-loaded agent networks driving acquisition cost into the premium | Digital-first onboarding plus a tooled agent ecosystem; commission loading engineered down | Digital-first |

**So what:** the human-touch layer is the bulk of a traditional insurer's
expense loading on long-duration business. Automating it is what turns
unaffordable long-term cover into an accessible product — planning basis:
expense ratio improving ~1.5pp annually through AI automation (adjustable in
the Scenario Lab). Humans stay exactly where they add judgment: edge-case
underwriting, complex claims, and governance — **AI recommends, the ledger
decides, people supervise**.
