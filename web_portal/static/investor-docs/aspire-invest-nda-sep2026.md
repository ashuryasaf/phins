## 1. Purpose

The parties are evaluating a **potential strategic relationship** —
including distribution, investments-desk diligence, and capital-markets
orientation — in connection with PHINS's technology platform and its
first insurance product. The Purpose does **not** include a priced
investment, a fundraising ask, or a bidding process. Those, if any,
require a separately scoped conversation and documents.

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means non-public information a party discloses
in connection with the Purpose, including:

- the PHINS platform architecture, append-only hash-chained ledger
  design, AI / automation specifications, and source code;
- actuarial models, the actuarial simulator, filed tables, and the
  three-year scale-scenario identity (including the 250,000 issued-
  policy planning target);
- the PHINS Unified Insurance Contract wording, pricing, and the Israel
  regulatory application memorandum;
- business plans, meeting outlines, counterparties, and milestone maps
  in this pack;
- Aspire-Invest non-public information about distribution capacity,
  investment processes, clients, or pipeline;
- customer, claims, or personal data, if any is shown.

Information is Confidential Information whether or not it is marked
confidential.

## 3. Obligations

Each receiving party shall:

- use Confidential Information **solely for the Purpose**;
- protect it with at least reasonable care and restrict access to
  personnel and professional advisers with a need to know who are bound
  by like obligations;
- not reverse-engineer, copy for a competing product, or disclose it to
  third parties without prior written consent;
- not treat anything in the meeting pack as a solicitation to buy or
  sell securities, a commitment to issue policies, or a licensed
  insurance quotation.

## 4. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes
public through no fault of the receiving party, was rightfully known
before disclosure, is independently developed without use of the
disclosing party's information, or must be disclosed by law, regulation,
or a competent authority (with prior notice where legally permitted).

## 5. No licence; no ask; return

No licence, option, or ownership is granted. **This Agreement is not a
term sheet and contains no fundraising ask and no bidding price.** Upon
request or termination, the receiving party will return or destroy
Confidential Information (except archival copies required by law or
professional standards) and certify the same on request.

## 6. Residual knowledge

Residual unaided memory of general concepts is not a licence to use
Confidential Information in a competing product. Trade secrets remain
protected while they remain trade secrets.

## 7. Term

This Agreement remains in effect for **2 years** from the Effective Date.
Confidentiality obligations survive for **5 years** after each
disclosure (and indefinitely for trade secrets while they remain such).

## 8. Remedies

A breach may cause irreparable harm. The disclosing party may seek
injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy, without prejudice to
damages.
