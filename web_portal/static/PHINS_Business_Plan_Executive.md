## Executive Summary

**PHINS** (Personal Health Insurance & Savings) is revolutionizing personal financial protection by combining severe disability insurance with wealth-building investments in a single, AI-powered contract.

- **1 in 4 adults** will experience disability before retirement (SSA)
- **$70,000/year** average cost of severe disability care
- **$3+ trillion** global disability insurance market
- **Traditional insurance leaves critical gaps** in long-term care coverage
