Section 1 — Company Overview & Platform Summary
1. Company Overview & Platform Summary
PHINS Insurance Ltd. — Platform Description and Business Model for the Albanian Market

PHINS (Personal Health Insurance & Savings) is an insurtech company operating a comprehensive, AI-powered insurance and savings platform entering the Albanian market. The company targets the significant 72% health insurance gap in Albania's approximately €300 million insurance market, serving the 2.8 million population that largely lacks adequate private health coverage. With insurance penetration at only ~1.5% of GDP, Albania represents a high-growth emerging market opportunity.

4. Business Description & Product
ADL Disability Coverage — Product Architecture and Actuarial Model for Albania
Flagship Product: ADL Disability Coverage

The PHINS flagship product is an Activities of Daily Living (ADL) long-term disability insurance policy tailored for the Albanian market. ADL coverage pays a lump-sum benefit when the insured is unable to perform a defined number of ADL activities (eating, dressing, bathing, mobility, continence, and transferring), providing financial security for individuals and families in Albania where private health coverage is extremely limited.

7. Claims Reserve Projections
Reserve Adequacy Analysis — IBNR, Catastrophe Reserve, and Coverage Ratios (€ Millions)
Reserve Policy: PHINS maintains actuarially certified claims reserves consistent with AFSA's capital adequacy framework under Law No. 52/2014. Reserves include: (a) Incurred But Not Reported (IBNR) at 25% of expected annual claims; (b) Catastrophe reserve for tail events; and (c) target reserve coverage ratio ≥150%. Initial solvency capital of €1.5M secured.

Reserve Methodology

Expected Claims

Calculated using actuarially certified ADL disability incidence tables adapted for Albanian demographics, policy mix, and in-force exposure. Grows proportional to policy count.

IBNR Reserve

Incurred But Not Reported reserve set at 25% of expected annual claims. Covers claims with reporting lags typical in disability insurance in emerging markets.

️

Catastrophe Reserve

Maintained for tail risks including seismic events (Albania is earthquake-prone), pandemic scenarios, and mass casualty events. Set conservatively at €0.04–€0.18M.

Reserve Adequacy: The reserve coverage ratio declines from 200% (Year 1) to 157% (Year 3) as the portfolio scales. All coverage ratios exceed the minimum 150% target and AFSA regulatory minimums. The actuarial reserve is reviewed annually by an independent qualified actuary. Results are included in annual statutory filings with AFSA.

Regulatory Compliance

All reserve calculations comply with Law No. 52/2014 "On Insurance and Reinsurance Activity", AFSA circulars on reserve adequacy, and IFRS 17 (Insurance Contracts) as applicable to Albanian insurance companies. Initial solvency capital of €1.5M has been secured.

Section 10 — Risk Factors
10. Risk Factors
Key risks specific to the Albanian market that prospective investors should consider

Investment in securities involves risk. The following risk factors are not exhaustive and reflect conditions specific to the Albanian market. Investors should read this section carefully and consult their own advisers before making any investment decision.

Regulatory Risk

PHINS operates under the supervision of three Albanian regulatory authorities (AFSA, Bank of Albania, Albanian Securities Commission). Changes to Law No. 52/2014, Law No. 9879/2008, or AFSA guidelines may adversely affect operations, capital requirements, or product offerings.

Emerging Market Risk

Albania is an emerging market with lower insurance penetration (~1.5% of GDP). Limited consumer awareness of private insurance products may slow adoption. Economic volatility and GDP fluctuations could reduce disposable income for insurance purchases.

Currency Risk

Premiums are denominated in EUR and converted to ALL at ~107. Significant EUR/ALL exchange rate movements may affect revenue, pricing competitiveness, and reserve adequacy. Albania is not yet in the Eurozone.

Technology Risk

The business model is highly dependent on AI underwriting and automation systems. Albania's digital infrastructure in some regions may limit platform accessibility. System failures or cybersecurity breaches could disrupt operations.

Capital & Liquidity Risk

Year 1 projects a net loss of €0.14M. If proceeds are insufficient or customer acquisition is slower than projected in Albania, additional capital may be required. Initial solvency capital of €1.5M must be maintained per AFSA requirements.

EU Accession Risk

Albania is an EU candidate country. The pace and terms of EU accession may significantly alter the regulatory landscape, requiring substantial compliance adjustments. Conversely, delays in accession could limit market confidence.

AML / KYC Compliance Risk

Albania has strengthened its AML framework under Law No. 9917/2008. PHINS must maintain robust KYC procedures. Albania's inclusion on international monitoring lists could affect banking relationships and investor confidence.

️ Seismic & Catastrophe Risk

Albania is located in an active seismic zone (2019 earthquake caused significant damage). A major seismic event could trigger widespread disability claims and infrastructure disruption exceeding catastrophe reserves.
