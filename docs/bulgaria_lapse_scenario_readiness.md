# Bulgaria — lapse-scenario market readiness (private and public-funded)

> **Status:** Research only. Public evidence is source-tagged. Scenario Lab TAM
> numbers are planning assumptions and are **not** reported market facts.
> **Audience:** Founder, investor meeting, FSC / NSSI / NHIF counterparties.
> **Method:** Same outlines as the PHINS Scenario Lab lapse frame
> (`web_portal/static/pitch-dashboard.html` market evidence + readiness
> scorecard). Readiness = `0.4 × operating fit + 0.3 × private access + 0.3 × demand`.
> **As-of:** September 2026. 2025 official statistics were still published in
> BGN at the irrevocable euro rate of **BGN 1.95583 = EUR 1**.

---

## 0. What this brief answers

The Scenario Lab lapse thesis is that **pay-as-you-go national disability /
LTC / pension schemes are under predicted collapse** (ageing, falling
worker-to-beneficiary ratios, benefit erosion), while private carriers have
retreated from lifelong severity cover. PHINS “fence” solutions are the
**severity-layer fence** — life with a lifelong 3+ ADL permanent-disability
mechanism, plus care-navigation / vocational-rehab overlays — that sits
between a stressed public scheme and a thin private book. They are **not**
a replacement for NSSI or NHIF, and they are **not** an MTPL price product.

This note checks whether Bulgaria is compatible with that fence, separately
for the **private** commercial market and the **public-funded** social
system, on five lab outlines:

1. Technology readiness
2. Premiums actually paid
3. Companies / infrastructure
4. Average risk-age distribution
5. Who to compare, compete, or collaborate with

**Headline.** Bulgaria is an **amber top-up / partner** market, not a
launch-ready replacement market. Demand and public-system depth are
stronger than Portugal’s; private monetization and life/LTC access are
weaker. Composite readiness scores **63** — the same band as Portugal —
but the *shape* is different: more public pressure, less private premium
to capture.

| Scorecard input | Bulgaria | Portugal (lab) | Sweden (lab) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Demand | 82 | 74 | 88 |
| Monetization | 48 | 56 | 52 |
| Public depth | 88 | 58 | 93 |
| Private access | 44 | 54 | 46 |
| Operating fit | 63 | 62 | 70 |
| Priority | 66 | 64 | 74 |
| **Readiness** | **63** | **63** | **68** |
| Stage | Top-up / partner | Top-up / orchestration | Top-up / orchestration |

## 1. Lapse-scenario outlines applied to Bulgaria

### 1.1 Public-funded system (the scheme that can lapse)

Bulgaria’s public fence is already built. It is deep, mandatory, and
fiscally heavy:

| Layer | Who pays / who runs | What is covered | 2025/26 evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| State pension + disability + survivors | NSSI (НОИ), PAYG Pensions Fund | Old-age, disability due to general illness, survivors | **2,061,409** pensioners in Oct 2025; average pension **BGN 1,006.92 / month**; pension outlays **BGN 19,859.2 million** for Jan–Oct 2025 (82.4% of the annual plan) |
| Occupational accident / disease | NSSI Occupational Accident Fund | Work injury (statutory) | Recalculated occupational-injury pensions are the one disability class NSSI reports as *falling* (929 in 2025 vs 962 in 2024 in the cited bulletin cut) |
| Health | NHIF (НЗОК), 8% contribution on insurable income | Statutory health | Contribution is mandatory for every employee; not a commercial premium |
| Pillar 2 supplementary pension | Licensed universal pension funds (private managers, mandatory for people born after 1959) | Additional old-age | 5% contribution; this is **funded**, not PAYG, but it is still public-mandated |
| Insurer insolvency backstop | Guarantee Fund Security Fund | MTPL/passenger accident in full to the statutory minimum; life receivables up to **BGN 196,000** per person | Activated as a live political fact after the DallBogg withdrawal |

Social-security contribution stack on gross pay (2025): **32.70–33.40%**
total (employer 18.92–19.62%, employee 13.78%), of which pension
14.8–19.8%, pillar 2 = 5% (born after 1959), health **8%**. Insurable
income was capped at BGN 4,130 / month from April 2025.

**Lapse pressure is demographic, not legal.** NSI final 2025 data:

- Population **6,423,207** (−14,153 / −0.22% vs 2024)
- Aged 65+ **1,557,851** = **24.3%** (Vidin 31.6%, Sofia-city 19.2%)
- Under 15: **891,674** = 13.9%
- Working-age (legislative): **3,764,842** = 58.6%
- Over working-age: **1,700,684** = 26.5%
- Mean age **45.4** (urban 44.6, rural 47.7)
- NSI age-dependency (under 15 + 65+ per 100 aged 15–64): **61.6%**
- Eurostat old-age dependency: **38.7%** at Dec 2025 (record in the
  published series)
- Demographic replacement (entrants 15–19 per 100 exits 60–64): **75**
- Crude birth rate **7.8‰**

NSSI’s own 2025 bulletin records a **rising share of disability-due-to-
general-illness** among *recalculated* pensions (31% → 33% → **35%**
over 2023–2025) and a 23% three-year rise in working disability
pensioners. A clean **national stock** of disability pensioners was
**not** published in the sources used here — that gap stays a gap.

**Public-sector compatibility for a PHINS fence**

- Compatible as a **top-up / navigation layer**, not as a statutory
  substitute. Same rule the lab already uses for Sweden, Germany, and
  Japan.
- Compatible as a **group / employer** attach on top of the 8% NHIF
  contribution (voluntary health already grew ~23% in 2025).
- Compatible as a **public-procurement adjacency** only where the buyer
  is a ministry, municipality, or SOE buying property / staff benefits —
  OZK already lives in that channel. Do not treat NHIF or NSSI as a
  distribution partner without a written public-law basis.
- **Not** compatible as a replacement for occupational-accident cover
  (that risk is already socialized) or as a bid against the Guarantee
  Fund.

### 1.2 Private commercial market (the book that can pay)

FSC figures as relayed by XPRIMM for FY2025:

| Book | GWP | Δ y/y | Share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Total | **BGN 5.25 billion** (EUR 2.68 billion) | +12% | 100% |
| Non-life | BGN 4.38 billion | +12.16% | 83.4% |
| Life | **BGN 870 million** (EUR 445 million) | +11.36% | 16.6% |
| Property (fire & elemental) | BGN 542.13 million | +24.3% | — |
| Claims paid (all) | BGN 2.54 billion | +25% | — |
| Property claims | BGN 176.8 million | ~×2 | — |

Supporting structure (ABZ / Economic.bg RATE, July 2026):

- Motor still ≈ **70%** of the general-insurance portfolio; XPRIMM’s
  older portfolio template put overall motor at **61%** of the whole
  market.
- Home insurance ≈ **10%** of dwellings — last in Europe (ABZ).
- Insurance penetration ≈ **2.3% of GDP** (NSI 2025 GDP EUR 116.018
  billion; FSC GWP EUR 2.68 billion). EU average is roughly double.
- 36 locally licensed insurers at the end-2024 FSC census (26 non-life /
  health, 10 life). ~434 EU/EEA freedom-of-services notifications by
  March 2026.
- Sector assets ≈ EUR 7.69 billion at end-March 2026 (BNB via SeeNews).
- Trend / ABZ May 2026 survey: trust in insurance **60%**; voluntary
  cover interest rising; awareness still the constraint.

**DallBogg is the 2026 pricing event.** FSC withdrew the licence of
ZAD DallBogg: Life and Health on 9 June 2026 (new-business ban from
2 April). The company had become one of the largest MTPL writers
(~900,000 customers; ~BGN 432 million / EUR 220 million 2025 GWP).
FSC cited a ~EUR 280 million reserve hole and Solvency II MCR breach.
Deputy chair Plamen Danailov’s public line: years of below-cost MTPL
pricing will now re-price onto the rest of the market, with the
Guarantee Fund absorbing a large unpaid-claims tail.

That is a **persistency / trust lapse**, not a mortality lapse. It
helps a severity-layer entrant (the market is being forced back to
economic price) and hurts a motor-led entrant.

**Private-sector compatibility for a PHINS fence**

- Compatible as a **life + disability + employer-health** attach,
  especially through bancassurance (DZI / UBB / KBC) and group health
  (Generali).
- Compatible as a **digital MGA / fronting** overlay on a VIG, Generali,
  Allianz, or Groupama balance sheet.
- **Not** compatible as a greenfield MTPL or casco play. That book is
  scale-and-loss-ratio, and 2026 is a clean-up year.
- Life GWP of EUR 445 million is the honest commercial pool for a
  fence. Do not add NSSI pension outlays or NHIF contributions to it.

## 2. Readiness by outline

### 2.1 Technology

| Signal | Evidence | Readiness |
|---|---|---|
| Supervisory stack | EU member; Solvency II; eurozone since **1 January 2026**; DORA in force since 17 January 2025; FSC Innovation Hub live | High — same operating system as Portugal |
| Core PAS / digital retail | DZI (KBC) awarded “Best Digital Insurance Company – Bulgaria” (2025): B2C portal, UBB-mobile attach, AI claims, self-inspection. Groupama called out for insurtech posture. Allianz Life runs a centralized online PAS | Medium-high at the international groups; uneven at domestic motor writers |
| Distribution tech | Boleron and other aggregators already quote MTPL / casco / travel online. Broker reporting is an FSC statistical series | Medium — digital for motor, thin for life/disability |
| Public digital rails | NSSI is actively exchanging digital-process experience (Poland ZUS, March 2026). ESGRAON is the civil-registration spine NSI already uses | Medium — identity rails exist; they are not an insurance API |
| Regulatory drag | Academic scan of 34 non-life + 14 life entities: almost no product is *fully* online; obstacles are legal (wet signature / ID) as much as technical | Medium-low for a remote ADL product |
| Post-DallBogg ICT bar | DORA + FSC ICT contact point + Solvency II data quality will be non-negotiable for any new license or MGA bind | Fail-closed — a feature for PHINS, a cost for a thin local start-up |

**Read for PHINS:** the fence should enter as **software on someone else’s
licence**, not as a 36th local PAS. DZI/KBC and Groupama are the
technology peers; Boleron is a motor-quote rail, not a severity UW rail.

### 2.2 Premiums paid

Reported 2025 commercial premiums (FSC / XPRIMM):

| Line | Premiums paid / written | What it is **not** |
|---|---|---|
| All insurance | BGN 5.25bn GWP | Not PHINS TAM |
| Life | BGN 870m GWP | Not an LTC book; mix includes unit-linked and savings |
| Property | BGN 542m GWP | Household + corporate + agri; ~10% of homes |
| Health (voluntary) | Growing ~23% (ABZ / RATE); no clean FSC line split surfaced here | Not NHIF’s 8% |
| Motor | ~70% of non-life | The opposite of a fence product |

Public amounts that **must stay out of the premium pool**:

- NSSI pensions Jan–Oct 2025: BGN 19.86bn
- Planned full-year NSSI spend path: ~BGN 27.4bn if the 81.8%
  year-to-date execution holds (planning identity only; not used as GWP)
- NHIF 8% levy on capped insurable income

Implied 2025 life premium per working-age person
(`870e6 / 3,764,842`) = **BGN 231 / year** (EUR 118). That is a
**penetration identity**, not a product price. The Scenario Lab
lives-mode premium is the actuarial kernel quote at age 45:
**EUR 1,170** (ILS 4,680 × pinned EUR/ILS 0.25). It is not an FSC
average and it is not the implied BGN 231 life spend.

**Read for PHINS:** households already pay a large *public* premium
(pension + health + pillar 2). Willingness to add a *private* fence is
proven only in employer health and, more slowly, property. Price the
fence as a **group / bancassurance add-on**, not as a retail life
ticket competing with BGN 231 / year of existing life spend.

rails. Domestic motor writers have volume and a 2026 trust problem. A
fence belongs on the first barbell.

### 2.4 Average risk-age distribution

Official NSI 31.12.2025 age stack (this is the risk-age distribution;
there is **no** published private-LTC age curve):

```
         0–14          15–64 (derived)       65+
      891,674            3,973,682         1,557,851
        13.9%               61.9%             24.3%

Working-age (legal definition, sex-specific retirement ages):
      3,764,842 (58.6%)     male 1,967,204 / female 1,797,638

Mean age of the population: 45.4
PHINS Israel specimen age:  42
```

Eurostat old-age dependency **38.7** vs Portugal lab **37.1** — Bulgaria
is slightly older on the 65+ / 15–64 measure and *much* older on NSI’s
broader 61.6 dependency ratio because the child share is also low.

Geographic concentration of risk age:

- Oldest districts: Vidin 31.6% 65+, Smolyan 31.1%, Gabrovo 30.8%
- Youngest: Sofia-city 19.2%, Varna 21.7%
- 35.8% of the population lives in the six cities >100k
- Northwest is emptying; Southwest (Sofia) is the only growing region

**Read for PHINS:** the national mean age (45.4) sits almost on the
Israel specimen (42). That is good for a working-age disability fence.
The **lapse** (public-scheme) pressure lives in the 24.3% aged 65+ and
in the 2.06 million NSSI pensioners — a top-up / care-navigation story,
not an issue-age 70 retail story. Issue-age discipline should stay
**3–65**, same as Israel Draft 3.1. Do not file a post-65 retail fence
until a Bulgarian appointed actuary has replaced the Israel lapse and
incidence tables.

A country-specific lapse table was **not** surfaced. Until one is, keep
the Israel 8% planning lapse *out* of any Bulgarian filing and treat
it as a foreign scenario input.

## 3. Scenario Lab planning layer (not public evidence)

Pinned so the dashboard and this memo cannot drift. Lives-mode premium is
the **actuarial kernel** (`risk_reference_v1`, published tables life 0.25 /
disability 0.20, ILS 1,000,000 / 250,000 face) at Bulgaria's official mean
age 45.4 → **45** (`f=1.300` → ILS 4,680), converted at the pinned planning
FX EUR/ILS = 0.92 / 3.68 = 0.25 → **EUR 1,170**. That quote is Layer B.
It is not an FSC average and it does not replace the Israel book (age 42 /
ILS 4,518).

| Input | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Addressable lives | **3,764,842** | NSI working-age stock 31.12.2025 (legal retirement ages) |
| Attach rate | 1.2% | Conservative vs Portugal 1.8% — thinner private life |
| Annual premium | **EUR 1,170** | Kernel quote at age 45. **Not** a filed price and **not** the FY2025 life GWP |
| Market premium pool | EUR 444,824,000 | FY2025 life GWP BGN 870m / 1.95583 (premium-share mode only) |
| Target share | 2.0% | Planning |
| Cession | 22% | SII planning; replace with a quote |
| Loss ratio | 60% | Planning |
| Expense ratio | 18% | Planning (higher than DZI-class; digital still has ID-friction) |
| Lives TAM (lab) | ≈ EUR 52.9m | `3,764,842 × 1.2% × 1,170` |
| Premium-mode TAM (lab) | ≈ EUR 8.9m | `444,824,000 × 2.0%` |

Use **lives mode** as the default, consistent with other ageing / public-depth
markets. The 65+ stock (1,557,851) stays in the *demand* column; it is
not the issue-age pool. The retired EUR 720 stub was an income-adjusted
guess and is no longer used.

## 4. Compatibility verdict

| Question | Private sector | Public-funded sector |
|---|---|---|
| Is there a lapse to fence? | Yes — life is 16.6% of GWP, homes 10% insured, motor crowds out income protection. DallBogg is a trust lapse. | Yes — 24.3% aged 65+, 2.06m pensioners, pension cash outlays already ~BGN 20bn by October, disability-general-illness share of recalculations at 35% and rising. |
| Can PHINS replace the incumbent? | No. Enter as MGA / white-label / bancassurance. | No. NSSI / NHIF are statutory. Fence is top-up, navigation, group attach. |
| Who holds the door? | DZI/KBC, VIG, Generali, Allianz, Groupama | OZK (procurement), ministries/municipalities as *buyers of staff benefits*, FSC Innovation Hub as *supervisor* |
| First product | Working-age 3+ ADL + life, group/bank attach, EUR pricing | Same product sold as a complement to NHIF / NSSI, never as a substitute |
| Hard stop | No MTPL, no below-cost pricing, no Israel lapse table in a Bulgarian filing | No claim to administer NSSI disability; no unsolicited use of NHIF data |

**Go shape:** partner-led CEE eurozone top-up, sequenced *after* the
Israel proof and *beside* Portugal as a second SII gateway — not instead
of it. Bulgaria is younger in private-premium terms and older in
public-scheme stress. That is exactly the lapse-scenario fence.

## 6. Integrity contract (same four rules as the lab)

1. **Local currency of the evidence year.** 2025 FSC and NSSI figures
   stay in BGN; 2026 operations are EUR. The conversion 1.95583 is the
   official irrevocable rate, not a market FX guess.
2. **Public is not premium.** NSSI outlays and the 8% NHIF levy validate
   demand. They are never added to GWP.
3. **Missing stays missing.** No national disability-pensioner stock, no
   private LTC insured-lives count, no disability cession ratio, no
   country lapse table.
4. **TAM is a scenario.** EUR 52.9m lives-mode (kernel EUR 1,170) /
   EUR 8.9m premium-mode are lab outputs. They do not appear in the
   evidence table as facts.
5. **Lives premium is the kernel.** EUR 1,170 is `risk_reference_v1` at
   age 45, FX-converted. It is not written into the FSC / NSI / NSSI
   evidence strings. The Israel book stays ILS 4,518 at age 42.
